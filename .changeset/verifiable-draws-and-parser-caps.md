Verifiable draws and parser limits (strategy contract 1.2.0, SECURITY_AUDIT F7 and F15). `runTally` no longer takes a `seed`: the recorded seed is derived from the round, strategy and recorded inputs (`deriveDrawSeed`, SHA-256 under the `decisionator/draw-seed/v1` tag), so anyone can recompute it and the owner cannot try seeds until a preferred option wins; re-deciding with unchanged votes repeats the draw. New `verifyOutcomeSeed` (`no-draw` / `verified` / `unverifiable`, never throws; outcomes recorded with earlier CSPRNG seeds show as legacy / unverifiable), `drawSeedMaterial`, `canonicalJson` and `redecidedCount`. The Rng and its test vectors are unchanged. The decide screens show whether the outcome in force has a verifiable draw and how many times its round was re-decided. `extractOptionsJson` refuses text over 1 000 000 characters and finds fenced blocks and citation tokens with linear scans instead of regexes that were quadratic on unclosed fences. `readWorkbook` inflates only the XML parts of an `.xlsx`, rejects parts over 25 MB and workbooks over 50 MB uncompressed (declared sizes before inflating, actual sizes after). The web app's storage manager resolves stores by exact full or short id only, no longer by id suffix.
